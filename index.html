A community-owned early warning system for HR 1. It watches for smoke — overdue renewals, paperwork losses, people giving up — so we can act before people lose CalFresh and Medi-Cal they still qualify for.

Data window: Sept 2026  ·  Refreshed monthly
— Why a smoke detector

A fire alarm tells you who already lost coverage. A smoke detector warns you while you can still act.

Most coverage loss under HR 1 will come from paperwork, not ineligibility. In Arkansas, more than 95% of people subject to work requirements already met them or should have been exempt. Each indicator below has thresholds set by the community, and it raises its level before losses show up in official counts.

ClearWithin normal range. Keep watching.
HazeDrifting. Work group checks the cause.
SmokeAct now. Brief the Steering Committee.
FirePeople are losing help. All hands.

How it works

Civic Signals is community-maintained and community-owned. It joins two streams, what people report and what the systems count, and turns them into early warnings and a public dashboard.

1 · Gather

Signals come in

  • Signal form in English and Spanish
  • Text message and QR codes at pantries, clinics, barbershops
  • 211 referral follow-ups
  • Monthly county and health plan counts
2 · Check

Community researchers

  • Paid researchers with lived experience review every signal
  • Remove names and details that identify anyone
  • AI helps group signals; people confirm each pattern
3 · Detect

Readings vs. thresholds

  • Eight indicators, refreshed monthly
  • Thresholds set and changed by the Steering Committee
  • Levels: clear, haze, smoke, fire
4 · Act

Alerts and the dashboard

  • Smoke or fire alerts to work group leads by email and text
  • Public dashboard on civicsignals.net
  • Monthly one-page brief for BRIDGE

Rules that protect people

  • Counts, not cases. Only totals leave partner systems.
  • Small groups stay hidden. Any cell under 11 people is suppressed.
  • No immigration status is ever collected.
  • Quotes are shared only with consent, and with identifying details removed.
  • The community owns the data. Partners sign a data agreement; the Steering Committee approves every new source.
